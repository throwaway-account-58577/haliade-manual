HaliadeOS has another declarative system called "Houses".
This is Haliade's way to do home management.

Houses are a declarative and reproducible way to replicate how your system looked like, or what was in the system.
including dotfiles, scripts, config files, or downloaded media.

The system house is stored at /etc/houses/house.lua
But can also be local to each user at their ~/.config. (~/.config/houses/house.lua)
(you can access the example house config by doing ctrl + alt + h in your shell)


The house focuses on environment, like your living room.
The house allows you to :

-- declare config files
-- declare scripts
-- run commands (like git clone or more)
-- download files from the internet

so you can replicate your setups perfectly, and share them with everyone, accross every Haliade OS system,
no matter their init, packages, or anything else that's different from your system.

(when synchronizing the house of someone else, remember to check what's inside,
if a house breaks your Haliade system, the development team is not responsible for the damage done.)

to synchronize your house, use the "house-synchronize" command.

As always, learn more inside the example file.
